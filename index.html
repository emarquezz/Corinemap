Interactive metabolic map

Explore and edit C. glutamicum metabolism.

Corinemap combines an editable Escher pathway with named flux datasets, categorical reaction styling, customizable metabolite appearance, and publication-ready export.

Open Corinemap Read the guide

Edit the pathway

Search, move, add, and edit reactions using the bundled iCW773 model.

Switch flux datasets

Move between Mannitol, Xylose, and imported CSV datasets without reloading.

Style live

Preview reaction, metabolite, font, size, and color adjustments directly on the map.

Save and publish

Reopen complete workspaces or export SVG and PNG figures for presentations and papers.

Corinemap runs entirely in your browser and is built on the Escher pathway editor.
